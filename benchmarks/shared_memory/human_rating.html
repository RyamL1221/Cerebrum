Human rating

This browser does not keep your progress on this page (its storage is off or full). Export your ratings before you close or reload the page.

Start

Pick the items file you were given (items.json) and type your rater code. Your ratings stay in this browser until you export them as a CSV file; export at the end of each session and send the file to the study's coordinator.

How to rate

  • Read the user's profile, the task context, the query and the answer, then give each of the three scores with the rubric's words. Score each dimension on its own.
  • Judge the answer as written. Do not look anything up and do not ask anyone about an item until every rater has finished.
  • If you cannot score an item, skip it and say why in the comment. A comment is optional otherwise.
  • The first items are a warm-up: they are not analysed.
  • Work in sessions of about 45 minutes. Your place is kept.

Keys outside the comment box: 1 to 5 give the current dimension its score and move on; Enter or n: save and next; b: back; s: skip; x: clear scores; c: comment (Esc leaves it); u: first unrated; e: export.
