# pilot_v1_graphiti: provenance

The pilot_v1 smoke on Graphiti (NeurIPS Subtask 16): the Subtask 11
pilot's locus contrasts (kernel vs replicated vs native) on a third
backend, for qwen2.5:7b at H = 0 and H = 2 on pilot_v1's 30 users. The
pilot's design: AIOS `docs/PILOT.md` and `docs/HETEROGENEITY.md`; the
backend: AIOS `docs/GRAPHITI_BACKEND.md` and `docs/NATIVE_USAGE.md`,
"native_graphiti". Result: AIOS `docs/DEV_STATUS.md`, Subtask 16.

`analysis.md` and `analysis.json` (with `cells.csv`, `cost.csv`,
`gaps.csv`) are `benchmarks.shared_memory.graphiti_smoke`'s output on
the six cells below, against the Subtask 11 pilot's gaps
(`results/pilot_v1_hetero/gaps.csv`). 180 user-trials, 0 failed. The
readings (the H = 0 tie and the direction of the H = 2 gap) are
reported, not required: `PILOT.md`'s rule is the pilot's.
